## Universal lifecycle

`inspect → classify → retrieve minimum context → execute → verify → route → update navigation → capture durable learning`

Use only the portion required by the task. Do not force a full project-management ceremony onto a bounded edit.

## Promotion lifecycle

`experimental → candidate → tested candidate → production → deprecated`

Production requires evidence of reuse, predictable quality, explicit dependencies, defined failure behavior, and a passing relevant evaluation.
